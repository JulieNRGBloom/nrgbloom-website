## Do not use

**Consent risk (4 files).** Several images are close-up group shots with
identifiable children and community members. There is no consent record in
the repo. Publishing them in commercial marketing is a genuine safeguarding
and privacy exposure, and some subjects are minors. Keep them out of posts
until written consent exists.

**Litigation-adjacent naming (9 files).** Files named `TON_*` date from the
TON Infrastructure period, and the filename alone would sit in page source.
Given the active dispute, do not publish these without a specific decision.

**Third-party marks (3 files).** `viabtc.webp` and the Gama logos are other
companies' trademarks. Only with their permission.

**AI or stock renders (7+ files).** Glowing Bitcoin coins over mountains, a
container in a river, a nuclear plant, a Montreal street, and a "250 MW
Campus" render that implies capacity you do not have. This is the legacy
retail-Bitcoin aesthetic and it undercuts a site whose pitch is sourced,
verified and operator-grade. Retire rather than reuse.

## Oversized, fix before reuse

8 assets exceed the 500KB page-weight limit from the v4 brief. The worst is
`images/nuclear-data-center.png` at 8830KB, which is also on the do-not-use
list. Compress before putting any of them on an indexable page.

## Gaps worth shooting

Nothing in the archive covers: the generator set running at night, the gas
intake or wellhead tie-in, the switchgear, anyone monitoring the site on a
laptop, or seasonal flooding. Those shots would carry the next six posts. One
site visit with a phone closes the gap.

## Rule for adding anything new

Every new asset gets a row here with its source and licence before it goes on
a page. Images get the same provenance discipline as statistics: if you cannot
say where it came from and that you are allowed to use it, it does not ship.
